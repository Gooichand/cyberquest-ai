# Quantum security research note

Quantum computing, post-quantum cryptography, and quantum machine learning are different topics. Post-quantum cryptography concerns algorithms intended to resist future quantum attacks. Quantum machine learning concerns learning procedures that use quantum circuits or quantum-inspired representations.

CyberQuest treats QML as experimental research. A classical state-vector simulation is not a quantum processor and cannot prove quantum advantage. Any comparison must include a classical baseline, synthetic-data limitations, metrics, and reproducible seeds.

Source: NIST post-quantum cryptography guidance and the CyberQuest research methodology.
